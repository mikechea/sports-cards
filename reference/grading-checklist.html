Five-step pre-grade

StepCheckWatch for
1 CenteringMeasure L/R and T/B borders, front and back. Ratio = bigger ÷ total.Back counts too. Worst axis sets the cap.
2 CornersLoupe on all four.A 10 needs four perfectly sharp corners. Slight fraying on one or two is about an 8.
3 EdgesRun your eye along all four edges.White chipping, especially on dark or chrome borders.
4 SurfaceTilt under a light.Scratches, print lines, dimples, stains, lost gloss.
5 MathGrade range with odds × sold comps per grade.Grade only if EV − (fee + shipping) > raw value.

Weakest link: the estimate is the lowest of the four areas, not the average. A PSA 9 allows only one minor flaw.

Worth-grading formula

EV = P(10)×PSA 10 comp + P(9)×PSA 9 comp + P(8)×PSA 8 comp
Grade if EV − (fee + shipping per card) > raw comp.
Rule of thumb: grading pays when a 10 is realistic and the 10 is worth far more than the 9. Don't forget the months-long wait.

Example: raw $45, 10/9/8 = $300/$80/$50, cost ≈ $68. At 52/48 and clean (40/50/10%), EV is $165, which beats raw by plenty. At 58/42 (5/60/35%), EV is $80.50, so sell raw.
