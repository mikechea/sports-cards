The drawer formula

Expectedstarting bank + cash sales − cash refunds − safe drops & paid-outs + paid-ins
Over / shortcounted − expected (negative = short)
Depositcounted − bank left for next shift

Rules: count blind (before seeing expected) · recount by denomination · overs are as bad as shorts · log every drop, paid-out, and variance · manager checks the count, a second person recounts big variances · per-cashier over/short log · learn the store's variance threshold (small shops often use $5–10).

Protect the case

One customer at a time · one item out at a time · lock the case right after every take-out and put-back · keys on your wrist, never on the counter · watch for distraction (a friend calling you away) · cameras cover the floor · high-value stock secured overnight.
