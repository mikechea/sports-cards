Before you price it

  • English and Japanese are different comps.
  • Base, alt-art, and Super Alt-Art of the same character are different comps.
  • Play price and art price are different comps.
  • Graded and raw are different comps. Customers will say PSA and CGC. CardVault's published dealer list is PSA, Beckett, and SGC.
