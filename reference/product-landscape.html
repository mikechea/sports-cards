Panini in 2026

StatusUnlicensed for the NBA (since Oct 2025) and the NFL (since spring 2026). No team logos, nicknames, helmets, or uniform trade dress.
Products2025-26 Prizm Basketball (Jul 31, 2026; 3 autos per hobby box; HOFers, NIL college players, pros on individual deals). 2026 Donruss Football (no NFL logos, a first for the brand). Prizm Draft Picks uses college/NIL rights.
PlayersPanini relies on individual player contracts. Sources conflict on whether it holds any NFLPA group rights: the NFLPA moved its exclusive to Fanatics in 2023, and Panini disputed that in arbitration.
ValueUnlicensed cards are generally discounted. Guides cite ~40–60%, but call it directional. Older licensed Prizm (2024 and earlier) is a separate market.
LawsuitPanini sued Fanatics for antitrust violations in 2023, and Fanatics countersued. In Aug 2026 Panini amended its complaint, alleging Fanatics used Panini's confidential license info. The case is in discovery, with trial likely in 2027 and settlement possible.

The bands are rough (from a single guide), and boxes often trade above list. Real examples: 2026 Topps Series 1 hobby ran ~$190–205 at retailers, and 2025 Topps Chrome Football hobby ~$600 (it launched with the NFL license).

Read the configuration line: "auto or relic" is not "auto," and "on average" is not "guaranteed." Pack and card counts change every release.

Rookie vs. 1st Bowman

RC (shield logo)Issued after the player's major-league debut.
1st BowmanThe player's first Bowman card, marked "1st" (upper left). Often years before the debut. Not an RC. The logo appeared in 1996 and became standard in 2003 (sources differ).
