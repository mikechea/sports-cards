STAR

PartShareSay
Situation~20%When, where, what went wrong. One or two sentences.
Task~10%What you owned.
Action~60%Specific steps, told with "I," not "we."
Result~10%A number or concrete outcome, plus what you'd repeat.

Keep it to 1.5–2 minutes. Rehearse the key points, not a script. Have stories ready for: covering a no-show, an upset person, delegating under time pressure, catching a mistake, and training someone.

The experience-gap answer

Own it ("I haven't held a key-holder title") → Map it (owned processes end to end, trained people, accountable when things broke, reported numbers, plus one STAR story) → Close it (studied opening and closing, drawer reconciliation, KPIs, comps, grading). Never inflate. "Not professionally. Here's the process I'd follow" beats a bluff.

Operational questions: the formula

Stay safe → follow store policy → communicate early (manager, team, customer) → document. Expect questions on open and close steps, a broken alarm, opening late, an upset customer, a no-show, delegating before close, motivating staff when it's slow, and POS.

Card-knowledge probes

QuestionAnswer core
Why $8 vs. $400?Player, rookie status, scarcity, condition
Who makes NFL/NBA cards?Topps (owned by Fanatics). NFL exclusive since Apr 2026 (Panini held it 2016–2026). NBA also moved from Panini to Topps.
What's it worth?Recent sold comps, same grade (130point, Card Ladder)
Should I grade it?Centering, corners, edges, surface, then compare cost to the price jump between grades
Buy counter?ID → condition → comps → offer below comps for margin → explain the number kindly
Pokémon or One Piece?Character, the exact printing (rarity symbol or Super Alt-Art vs. alt-art), English vs. Japanese comps. The box does not promise the chase. Lesson 8.

Questions to ask them

  • What does a typical opening-to-close shift look like for a Key Holder?
  • Which KPIs matter most in this role?
  • Who makes buy-counter offers, and what needs manager approval?
  • With 40 stores targeted by 2028, what's the growth path from Key Holder?
  • How is training done? Is there shadowing on grading submissions?
