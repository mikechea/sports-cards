Course · 8 lessons · ~10 minutes each

Sports Cards

From casual collector to confident behind the counter.

A short course for becoming a real collector and a trusted card-shop Key Holder. Sports cards are the core. Lesson 8 covers the Pokémon and One Piece the shop also sells. Each lesson teaches one skill, then makes you practice it with a quiz that gives instant feedback. Take them in order, and come back to a lesson's quiz a day or two later, when recalling it takes effort.

Lessons

  1. The Four Value Levers: player, rookie, scarcity, condition
  2. The 2026 Product Landscape: who makes what, and what to recommend
  3. Pricing With Comps: sold data, not asking prices
  4. Pre-Grading a Raw Card: centering, corners, edges, surface, and whether to grade at all
  5. The Buy Counter: fair offers, protecting cards, spotting fakes
  6. Key Holder Operations: open, close, reconcile, read the numbers
  7. Interview Prep: talk like a collector, answer like a leader
  8. Pokémon and One Piece: the other two products on the shelf
